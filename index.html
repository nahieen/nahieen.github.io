The rule behind everything

We build offline, local apps only.

Most software wants an account, a subscription, and a constant connection back to its maker. Nahieen Studios apps want none of that. They run on your computer, work without an internet connection, and keep your files and data on your machine. No cloud, no accounts — and nothing of yours is sent anywhere.

01

Honest about limits

Every app is upfront about what it does and what it doesn't do — the limits are on the page, not buried. OneScan, for example, says plainly: it's a scan helper, not an antivirus of its own.

02

Small on purpose

One job per app, done well. No feature bloat, no upsells, no settings you need a manual for.

03

Yours once it's yours

An offline app can't be switched off by a server shutdown or a changed subscription. It just keeps working.

Questions

Quick answers

Still stuck? Email the studio — one address handles everything.

Do the apps really work with no internet?

Yes. Every Nahieen Studios app runs entirely on your PC and works fully offline. You only need a connection to download the app itself from the Microsoft Store.

Are the apps free?

The apps on the Microsoft Store are free to download. No ads, no in-app upsells, no subscriptions.

What do I need to run them?

A Windows PC — the apps are built for Windows 10 and 11.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Everything you create or save stays on your computer, and the apps collect nothing — no personal information, tracking, analytics, or ads. The full privacy policy is right here on this page.

Can you build an app for me?

Possibly — custom work is taken online only, by email. Describe the task or the tool you wish existed, and you'll get a written scope back before anything starts. See Custom work above.
